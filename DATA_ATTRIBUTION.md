# Data attribution

The data shown by this project are U.S. public-domain statistics, reprocessed by this project's pipeline.

- **Public schools and districts:** U.S. Department of Education, National Center for Education Statistics (NCES), Common Core of Data (CCD). https://nces.ed.gov/ccd/
- **Private schools:** NCES Private School Universe Survey (PSS). https://nces.ed.gov/surveys/pss/
- **Postsecondary institutions:** NCES Integrated Postsecondary Education Data System (IPEDS). https://nces.ed.gov/ipeds/
- **Geocoding and boundaries:** U.S. Census Bureau Geocoder and TIGER/Line files. https://www.census.gov/
- **Basemaps:** OpenFreeMap, OpenMapTiles, (c) OpenStreetMap contributors.

NCES public-use data are for statistical purposes only; do not attempt to identify individuals.
Values may be suppressed, preliminary, or approximate; see the in-app Data page and dictionary caveats.
Nothing here ranks schools or implies quality.
